Sunday nights are an opportunity for our church family to gather, hear God's word, encourage one another and respond to the good news of Jesus. As a Service Leader, you'll help make that happen.

Service Leader · Role overview

Alongside a co-leader, you'll help lead a deep and warm Night Church service — filled with the truth of the gospel, and breathing the life of Christ.

First, the big picture

It's about more than running a service.

The way we lead matters. We want people to be able to engage with God's word, sing and pray together, and be encouraged to keep following Jesus. A thoughtful welcome, a clear introduction or a well-led moment in the service can help people take part and feel at home.

Deep Rooted in the truth of the Bible and the good news of Jesus.
Warm Genuine, welcoming and attentive to the people gathered.
Christ-centred Helping the whole service breathe the life of Christ.
What you'll do

You'll lead together, not on your own.

You'll be paired with a co-leader (most likely a pastor). Together, you'll prepare and lead the service, making sure each part fits together and helps the congregation engage.

01

Get ready

Spend time praying and understanding the Bible passage being preached, where it sits in the series, and the overall direction of the service.

02

Plan with your co-leader

Meet during the week to walk through the service plan and work out how you'll lead each part together — with warmth, clarity and good flow.

03

Lead on the night

Arrive ready for the team briefing, lead the service alongside your co-leader, and participate fully in the moments that bring the gathering together.

04

Reflect and keep growing

Take part in the debrief and any follow-up training. You'll have opportunities to reflect, learn and grow in your ability to lead.

You don't need to know it all already.

This role is a chance to give service leading a go, with someone alongside you. You'll have training with our Magnification Pastor and time to prepare with your co-leader before you lead. We want to help you grow in confidence and skill as you serve.

The practical details

A manageable commitment.

We're asking you to prayerfully consider leading around four to six services across the year. You'll be part of a Gathering Team, supported by its coordinator, and the Sunday service teams sit under the Magnification Pastor.
